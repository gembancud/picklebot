Decision: do not promote, automatically extend, or use this endpoint as a new baseline. Keep the common parent and no-shaping control. The reward-delivery hypothesis was verified, but the performance hypothesis was not supported by this single seeded experiment. Next investigate pre-contact failures versus outgoing-shot failures using matched trajectories before choosing another bounded learning intervention. Do not interpret isolated rightward successes as mastery or assume larger networks solve it.

The drill-mastery goal remains incomplete: directional generalization, reliable placement, retention across all required situations, repeated acceptance and representative final-model demos are not established. Final acceptance seeds remain unused. These are reused development anchors, not independent generalization or replicated causal evidence.
