# Phase 1B empirical-data request templates

These drafts ask only for material required by the normative calibration
specification. Review the recipient and sender details before sending.

## Measured flight traces

Subject: Request for pickleball trajectory coordinate exports

Hello,

I am building an open, reproducible pickleball simulation and would like to
validate its aerodynamic model against measured flight data rather than tune it
by eye.

Would you be willing to share the raw or processed trajectory coordinate
exports used in your pickleball aerodynamics study? The useful minimum is
time-stamped ball-centre coordinates together with each launch's initial
velocity, launch angle, spin magnitude/direction, ball model, ball mass and
diameter, frame rate, environmental conditions, and measurement uncertainty.
Original Tracker exports or CSV files are ideal.

Please also specify the licence or conditions under which the files may be
stored and cited in a public or private software repository. We will preserve
the original files, record SHA-256 hashes, cite the study, and clearly
distinguish measured data from simulator output.

Thank you.

## USA Pickleball drop-datum clarification

Subject: Clarification of the 78-inch pickleball bounce-test release datum

Hello USA Pickleball Equipment Evaluation team,

The current Equipment Standards Manual defines a 78-inch drop onto a granite
surface and measures the 30–34 inch rebound to the top of the ball. For a
reproducible physics fixture, could you please clarify whether the initial
78-inch release height is measured to the top, centre, or bottom of the ball?

If a public detailed ball-test procedure already resolves this datum, a link
and document revision/date would be greatly appreciated. We will cite the
answer verbatim only within normal quotation limits and record the date and
source; we are not seeking or claiming equipment certification.

Thank you.

## Acrylic-court laboratory or facility trace

Subject: Request for measured pickleball rebound traces on an acrylic court

Hello,

I am looking for reproducible pickleball impact measurements on a named rigid
acrylic court system. The required cases are vertical, shallow-angle, and spun
impacts, ideally at least five trials each.

Useful data include time-stamped ball-centre positions around impact, incoming
and outgoing linear velocity and spin, ball model/sample, surface system and
base construction, temperature, humidity, measurement method, uncertainty, and
raw-video or export hashes.

If such data already exist, may we use and cite the coordinate exports for
physics-model calibration? We will preserve provenance and will not represent
the measurements as certification of a ball, court, or paddle.

Thank you.
